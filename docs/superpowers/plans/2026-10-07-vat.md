# VAT / GST for every jurisdiction

Seeded 2026-10-07 for all 73 jurisdictions; evidence `unreviewed`. Research files (one
`<CC>.json` each: standard rate, valid_from, legal ref, quote, URL, reduced/other rates, enacted
future changes, notes, unverified) are curated by `vat/curate_vat.py` into
`app/modules/seed/data/vat/` and loaded by `app/modules/seed/vat.py` into `tax.vat_rule`.

- EU 27: standard rates all match the Commission's TEDB (snapshot 2026-07-01).
- No national VAT: US, HK, KW, QA, IQ, LY, SY (SY secondary — a draft sales-tax law not confirmed enacted).
- Secondary sources (official portals unreadable): KZ, LB, MD, SY, YE — evidence title says so.
- `future_changes` holds standard-rate changes only; reduced-rate changes (KZ, LV, SE) are moved
  into notes by the curate step.
- No start date found (open-ended period, date not shown): JO, MA, MD, YE and the no-VAT states.
- Other rates may include higher rates (IN 40%, PA 10%/15%), so the UI says "other rates".
