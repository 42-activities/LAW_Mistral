## Model additions (migration 0019)
- `tax.cit_refund`: shareholder refund of corporate tax on distribution (Malta 5/7 for passive
  royalties/interest, applied in the flow's CIT leg with a `shareholder_refund` review flag).
- CIT by income category (`domestic_tax_rule` CIT rows for ROYALTY/INTEREST), falling back to
  the general rate — Ireland 25% passive vs 12.5% trading.
- `treaty_rate.source_jurisdiction_id`: direction-specific caps (France–Germany dividends 0%
  from France, 5% from Germany; France–Ireland/Malta caps on French dividends only).
- `wht_exemption.reduced_rate` and several exemptions per income type (best qualifying wins).
- The CFC test now uses the holding's lowest effective rate on the profile's taxable income
  after refunds (Malta 35% → 10%).
