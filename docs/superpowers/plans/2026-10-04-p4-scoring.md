# P4 Scoring Implementation Plan

**Goal:** Rank candidate holding jurisdictions for a structured `Profile` with a reproducible,
fully sourced **ScoreCard** (spec §3): four independently computed factors, an explicit
`weight_set`, engine-side guardrails, persisted scoring runs, and a
`POST /v1/analyze/holding-recommendation` endpoint.

**Spec:** §3 (scoring model), §4 (`recommender` schema, `cfc_rule`, `substance_rule`), §8, §10.

## Global constraints

- Deterministic: same `(profile, candidates, weights, data_asof, engine_version)` ⇒ byte-identical
  ScoreCards (tested). Ties broken by jurisdiction code. `ENGINE_VERSION = "1.0.0"`; any change
  to a formula or constant below bumps it.
- Builds only on P3 engines; no new rate logic in the scorer.
- Missing data never becomes a good score: a candidate whose flows cannot all be computed has
  `overall_score = null`, `complete = false`, and ranks after every complete candidate.
- Migrations continue from **0014**. `ruff`, `mypy app`, `pytest` clean.

## Profile (input; P5's wizard will build it from tickable answers)

`parent` (ultimate parent jurisdiction), `flows` (income category, source jurisdiction, optional
annual amount), `holding_pct` (default 100), `holding_months` (default 24),
`substance_capacity` (`low|medium|high`), plus `activity` and `size` carried for P5.

## Factors (all 0–100, higher is better)

1. **Tax efficiency** — FlowCalculator leakage per flow (S → H → parent), averaged weighted by
   amount (equal weights when amounts are absent). `score = clamp(100 − 2 × leakage)`: 0 % → 100,
   9 % → 82, 25 % → 50, ≥ 50 % → 0. Flow citations and leg rates become `flow_breakdown`.
2. **Compliance** — 100 minus penalties for H's list memberships on `data_asof`:
   FATF black 100 · FATF grey 40 · EU Annex I 50 · EU Annex II 10 · EU AML high-risk 30 ·
   Global Forum `non_compliant` 40 / `partially_compliant` 20 · a national ETNC list of one of
   the profile's counterparties 50 (other national ETNC lists 10). Floor 0.
3. **Treaty breadth** — share of the profile's counterparties (flow sources ∪ parent, minus H)
   with which H has a treaty in force on `data_asof`. Computed live through `TreatyEngine`;
   the `treaty_coverage` materialized view is deferred until P8 data volume needs it.
4. **Substance burden** (coarse bands, spec §3):
   - requirement band from `tax.substance_rule` vs profile capacity: −30 per band of shortfall;
     no rule recorded → −30 and flag `substance_not_recorded` (unknown is not "no burden");
   - parent-country CFC (`tax.cfc_rule`) catches H (parent controls H and H's CIT is below the
     rule's threshold relative to the parent's CIT) → −40 and `cfc_exposure` flag,
     `interpretation_required` (safe harbours such as CGI 209 B III need professional review);
   - MLI principal purpose test on any treaty used by the flows → −10, flag.

Default weight set `default-v1`: tax 0.45 · compliance 0.20 · treaty 0.20 · substance 0.15
(spec §3 example). Custom weights are accepted per request, must be non-negative, are normalised
to sum 1, and are stored with the run.

## Guardrails (override the score, spec §3 hard rules)

- H on FATF black list → overall capped at **20**, flag `guardrail_fatf_black`.
- H on the national ETNC list of a profile counterparty → capped at **20**,
  flag `guardrail_etnc_counterparty`.

## Persistence (`recommender` schema, created in P0)

`weight_set(name unique, weights jsonb, is_default)`, `profile(org_id, answers jsonb, derived
jsonb, status, created_at)`, `scoring_run(org_id, profile_id null, profile_snapshot jsonb,
weight_set_id, weights jsonb, engine_version, data_asof, created_at)`, `scorecard(scoring_run_id,
jurisdiction_id, rank, overall_score null, complete, factor_scores jsonb, flow_breakdown jsonb,
guardrail_flags jsonb, citations jsonb)`. Plus `tax.cfc_rule` and `tax.substance_rule`
(versioned, exclusion-constrained, evidence FK).

## Tasks

1. Migration 0014 + models (recommender tables, `cfc_rule`, `substance_rule`), seed
   `default-v1` weight set and the France CFC rule.
2. `app/modules/scoring/` — `Profile`, factor functions, `Scorer.rank()` returning ScoreCards.
3. Persistence (`ScoringRunRepository.save`) and `POST /v1/analyze/holding-recommendation`.
4. Tests: factor unit tests, guardrail caps (synthetic FATF-black / ETNC memberships), weight
   normalisation, incomplete candidates rank last, reproducibility (two runs ⇒ identical JSON),
   France–UAE recommendation snapshot.

## France–UAE snapshot expectation (FR parent, FR-source dividends + royalties, 2026-10-01)

AE: tax efficiency high (0 % dividend leakage, 9 % royalty), compliance 100 (no listing on that
date), treaty breadth 100, substance penalised for French CFC exposure (9 % < 60 % × 25 %) and
unrecorded substance rules — exactly the trade-off the professional should see.
FR as its own holding: flows are domestic; FR is ranked on the same factors.

(flagged, not computed); usage metering of scoring runs (P7).
