# P8 batch 1 — Luxembourg, Netherlands, Cyprus (+ France EU-directive exemptions)

Sourced 2026-10-05 from official texts only (ACD/Legilux, wetten.overheid.nl/Belastingdienst,
cylaw.org/gov.cy, Légifrance/BOFiP, impots.gouv.fr treaty PDFs, european-union.europa.eu).
All evidence rows are `unreviewed` until a named reviewer confirms them. Data:
`app/modules/seed/batch_eu1.py`; golden tests: `tests/test_batch_eu1.py`.

## Model additions (migration 0018)
- `core.jurisdiction_group` (+ dated members): `EU`, `NL_DIV_TREATY`.
- `tax.wht_exemption`: domestic WHT exemption for recipients in a group with minimum holding %
  and months (EU directives). The engine reports the statutory rate as `domestic_rate` and the
  exempted rate as `effective_domestic_rate`, with an `exemption_anti_abuse` review flag.
- Tiered treaty rates (`treaty_rate.min_holding_days`; one row per ownership tier); the engine
  picks the lowest-capped tier whose conditions are met and explains skipped tiers.
- `regulatory_consequence.income_category_id` (NULL = all categories).
- `cfc_rule.threshold_inclusive`: France's 238 A test ("inférieur de 40 % ou plus") is
  inclusive, so a 15% holding regime (Cyprus 2026) is caught; LU/CY tests are strict.
